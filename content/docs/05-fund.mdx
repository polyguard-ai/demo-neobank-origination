## What\'s happening on this page

Before Beige Bank moves any money, we run a second Trust Check — this time with no document scan and no document re-issuance. We rebind to the original identity by requiring four proofs: PG-Presence and device attestation prove the person is physically at the device they verified with, while `name` and `pg_region` lock the re-verification to the same human and the same region as the KYC bundle.

This page also demonstrates a second SDK rendering style: instead of embedding into a target div, we let the SDK render its own full-screen modal overlay. We pass no `target` to `verify()` and the SDK appends a `<div>` to `document.body` with the QR, copy, and close/cancel buttons it owns.

```tsx
const reverify = new PolyguardClient({
  appId: process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_POLYGUARD_APP_ID,
  apiServer: 'api.polyguard.ai',
  requiredProofs: ['pg_presence', 'pg_attestation_key_id', 'name', 'pg_region'],
  scanType: 'multi',
});
// No target argument → SDK opens its own modal.
const bundle = await reverify.verify(undefined, /* rawJwt */ true);
```

When `verify()` resolves, the SDK has closed its modal but we haven't moved money yet — we still hold for the AES-256-GCM webhook receipt. The page flips to a "Confirming with Polyguard…" spinner, and the layout's `WebhookEnrichment` poller patches the store the moment `/api/status/{linkUuid}` returns the signed bundle. Only then does Beige Bank advance to the funded state.

The user experience is fast: typically under a second from tap to "Trust Check complete," plus another moment for the webhook.

## Why this is the actual fraud-prevention story

One-time KYC was designed for in-person banking. It assumed that once you knew who was opening the account, you'd know who was using it later. That assumption broke when accounts moved online — credentials get phished, stolen, sold, and reused.

**Continuous verification** rebinds the identity to the action. The person authorizing the wire today must be the same person who opened the account yesterday — proven on the same device, in real time, with PG-Presence.

## What about friction?

A Trust Check at funding sounds like adding friction. In practice, customers who just completed Polyguard Mobile onboarding don't feel a second Trust Check that takes ~1 second. They feel security. And they don't feel the chargeback Beige Bank just avoided.
