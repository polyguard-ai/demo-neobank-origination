## What\'s happening on this page

Nothing Polyguard-specific yet. Beige Bank is collecting the same details any neobank would: name, date of birth, email, and the last four of SSN. We store these in `localStorage` because this is a demo with no backend database.

## What is *not* happening

We are **not** checking the SSN against a credit bureau, doing OFAC/sanctions screening, or running velocity checks against an address. A real neobank would, and Polyguard sits alongside those checks — not in place of them. Polyguard's job is to confirm that the person submitting this form is who they say they are.

## A quick note on what we send to Polyguard

We do **not** forward this form data to Polyguard. Polyguard never receives the applicant's SSN or PII from us — the verification is keyed to the applicant's mobile device, not to the form contents. Privacy First architecture means Polyguard only knows what it needs to in order to verify identity.
