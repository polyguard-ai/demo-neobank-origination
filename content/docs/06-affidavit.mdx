## What\'s happening on this page

This is the compliance console — what Beige Bank's risk team and outside counsel see when they need to defend a verification in a dispute, regulator exam, or chargeback hearing.

The **Transaction Affidavit** is a signed PDF generated at the moment of verification and certified under NY CPLR § 3122-a, a New York rule that admits business records into evidence without requiring live testimony from the records custodian. In plain English: this PDF is the testimony.

## What\'s inside the affidavit

- Cryptographic hashes of the verified document.
- Biometric confidence score (typically 98%+).
- PG-Presence reading in milliseconds.
- Hardware attestation key id from Apple AppAttest or Google Play Integrity.
- Geographic region.
- ISO timestamp.
- JWKS-signed JSON Web Token attesting to all of the above.

The PDF itself lives on Polyguard infrastructure and is exposed to you as a presigned URL inside the webhook payload. Beige Bank never stores the affidavit — we just keep the URL.

## Why this matters operationally

Without an affidavit, fraud disputes become a he-said/she-said exercise: did Beige Bank do enough to verify identity? With one, the answer is a signed file with a timestamp and a certainty score. Reg E (unauthorized EFTs), CFPB exams, BSA/AML audits — they all run faster when you can hand over evidence instead of explaining process.
