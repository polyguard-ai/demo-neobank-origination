## What is this site?

This is **Beige Bank** — a fictional neobank we built so you can walk through a real account-origination flow that's protected by Polyguard.

Every page here has this guided tour panel. Open it on any step to read what's happening behind the scenes, and to copy the exact code your team would write to do the same thing.

## Why a demo for account origination?

The first 60 seconds of a customer relationship are the most expensive ones to get wrong. Neobanks lose more to fraud at signup than at any other moment in the customer lifecycle — and the attacks are evolving faster than legacy IDV vendors can respond.

In this tour you'll see:

- **Fused identity verification** — Polyguard confirms the person, document, device, and physical presence together, in one Trust Check.
- **PG-Presence** — optical distance bounding that closes the remote-access attack surface in roughly 100 milliseconds.
- **Privacy First architecture** — Beige Bank never receives biometric data. Polyguard never stores it.
- **Transaction Affidavit** — a court-admissible, NY CPLR § 3122-a certified record of every Trust Check.
